Our Story

Arya brings the rich culinary heritage of Persia to the heart of Manila. From charcoal-grilled kebabs and fragrant saffron rice to slow-simmered stews and warm, fresh pita, every dish is prepared with time-honored recipes and the warm hospitality Persia is known for.

Whether it's a casual lunch, a family gathering, or a celebration, Arya offers a welcoming space and flavors that are both hearty and memorable.

  • Charcoal-grilled kebabs
  • Saffron & herb rice
  • Slow-cooked stews
  • Fresh pita & dips
